Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16th October, 2026

Neone ("we", "us") is an online fashion store based in Nigeria. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this site, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your name and WhatsApp number, if you ask us to add you to our WhatsApp updates. You send these to us yourself in a WhatsApp chat. They do not pass through our website.
  • Order details you send us on WhatsApp: your name, phone number, delivery address, and the items, sizes and colors you want. You send these to us directly in a WhatsApp chat. They do not pass through our website.
  • Usage information from analytics cookies, such as the pages you view and the device and browser you use. We collect this only if you accept cookies.
  • Your order list. While you shop, your browser saves your order list so it is still there if you reload the page. It stays on your device.

Why we use it

  • To confirm availability, agree delivery and handle your order.
  • To send you updates about new arrivals on WhatsApp, if you asked to join. You can leave at any time by replying STOP.
  • To understand which products people like, so we can improve the store.

Cookies

We use browser storage to keep your order list and to remember that you agreed to our terms. We use analytics cookies (Google Analytics) only if you tap Accept on our cookie notice. If you decline, they are not loaded. You can also clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Delivery partners, limited to what is needed to fulfil and deliver your order, such as your name, phone number and delivery address.
  • Services we use: hosting and database providers, WhatsApp (which carries the messages you send us and our updates), and Google (analytics and fonts). Their servers may be outside Nigeria.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

Payments

We do not take card payments on our website (for now). You pay by bank transfer after we confirm your order. We will never ask for your card details or PIN.

Customer feedback

When we show a customer's feedback on a product page, we remove their name and phone number first.

How long we keep your information

We keep order details for 30 days after delivery so we can handle questions, complaints and refunds. We may keep basic sales records, such as the items bought, the amount and the date, for longer for business and tax purposes, but without your name, phone number or address. We keep your name and number on our updates list until you ask us to remove you.

Your rights

Under Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent. Contact us at neoneofficial1@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children

Neone is not meant for anyone under 18. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to place orders for you. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn that we have collected a child's information without a parent or guardian's consent, we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
