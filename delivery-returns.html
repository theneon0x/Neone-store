Delivery

  • We deliver nationwide across Nigeria.
  • The delivery fee depends on your location and your items. We confirm it on WhatsApp before you pay.
  • Because our items are limited, we confirm availability before you pay. If an item becomes unavailable after you have paid, we will offer a replacement or a full refund for that item.
  • Please check your delivery address and phone number. We cannot be responsible for delays caused by wrong details.

Check your order when it arrives

Please check your items as soon as you receive them. If something is wrong, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery and send clear photos or a short video of the item and its packaging.

Returns and refunds

We will replace the item, or refund you if a replacement is not possible, when:

  • you received the wrong item, meaning a different item from the one we confirmed with you;
  • the item is damaged or faulty when it arrives;

We do not accept returns or refunds for:

  • a change of mind, or a size you chose that does not fit; or
  • small differences between the AI-created first photo and the real item. The other photos show a more accurate look, so please check them before you order.

Items you send back must be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition. Refunds are paid by bank transfer within 5-7 working days of us approving the claim.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel before we dispatch your order. Message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible. Once an order has been dispatched, we cannot cancel it.
